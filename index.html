The platform

Opus Magnum is the vendor management system from Opus People Solutions. It carries a request for staff from the hiring manager who raises it, through approval, agencies, candidates and placement, to the shifts that are worked, the invoices that are raised and the files finance imports.

One record, from the first request to the final payment, so the client, the agencies and Opus are always reading from the same page.

Contents

    Who it serves

    Hiring managers

    Ask for staff in a few fields, see only the shortlist, and approve the people you will work with.

    Agencies

    Receive the work you are cleared for, when it is released to your tier, and see your self-bills line by line.

    MSP & compliance

    Approve rates against the cap, block what is not cleared, and keep every check running between bookings.

    Finance

    Invoices and self-bills from the same priced lines, and ledger, payroll and BACS files that are never exported twice.
